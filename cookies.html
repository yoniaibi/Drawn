Cookie Policy

Last updated: 18 June 2026  ·  Version 1.0

Short version: We use essential cookies to make the site work, and analytics cookies (only with your consent) to understand how people use DRAWN so we can improve it. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit — like whether you're logged in — so you don't have to re-enter it every time.

This policy covers cookies and similar technologies (like localStorage and session storage) used on the DRAWN website (drawn.app). The DRAWN mobile app does not use browser cookies.

3. Managing your cookie preferences

When you first visit DRAWN, you'll see a cookie banner asking for your consent to analytics cookies. You can change your mind at any time by clicking the "Cookie settings" link in the footer.

Browser-level controls

You can also control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Note: blocking essential cookies will prevent you from logging in to DRAWN.

Opt out of Google Analytics

You can opt out of Google Analytics across all websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Updates to this policy

We'll update this policy if we introduce new cookies or change how we use existing ones. If we start using analytics cookies from a new provider, we'll ask for your consent again.
