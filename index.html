Questions

Everything you want to know.

No hidden catches. Here's exactly how DRAWN works.

Is this gambling? Do I need to be 18+? +
DRAWN is a prize draw, not gambling. Every draw has a free entry route (no purchase necessary), which is what legally distinguishes it from a lottery or raffle. This is the same structure used by large charity draws and competitions across the UK. No gambling licence is required, and there's no age restriction — though we recommend 18+ for financial transactions.
What if a draw doesn't sell enough tickets? +
If a draw closes without reaching its minimum ticket threshold, all buyers are automatically refunded in full. The draw is cancelled and the seller keeps their item. No one loses out. Funds are held in escrow until the draw resolves — we never touch your money directly.
How do I collect if I win? +
Winning is handled entirely in the app. Once the wheel picks your name at 9pm, you get an instant notification and your item ships free within 2 business days. All items are held by DRAWN before going live — we verify them and dispatch directly. You never deal with the seller.
How are sellers paid? +
Sellers are paid within 24 hours of the winner confirming delivery. Payment goes directly to the bank account you link during onboarding. DRAWN takes a 12% platform fee — the rest is yours. For a £200 item at 25p/ticket with 2,000 tickets sold, a seller receives approximately £423.
Is the draw actually random? +
Yes — draws use a cryptographically secure random number generator, seeded at draw time. The outcome is provably fair and cannot be influenced by DRAWN, sellers, or any party. We're building a public audit log so anyone can verify past draws. The live wheel is purely visual entertainment — the winner is determined by the algorithm, not where the animation stops.
What can I sell on DRAWN? +
Anything fashion-adjacent that you no longer want: bags, shoes, watches, clothes, jewellery, accessories. Items must be authentic (we verify them), in the condition described, and worth at least £50 retail. Designer and luxury items work best — the bigger the prize, the more tickets sell and the more you earn.
How many tickets can I buy? +
Any single buyer can hold up to 25% of the tickets in any draw. This keeps draws fair — no one can dominate. If a draw has 2,000 tickets, the maximum you can buy is 500. At 10p each, that's £50 for a 25% shot at the prize.
When does the draw happen? +
Every night at 9pm UK time. All draws close simultaneously and resolve live in the app. You can watch the wheel spin for each draw in sequence — there's a live chat and reaction stream so the whole community watches together. Results and winner notifications go out by 9:15pm.
