4. How draws work

4.1 Ticket purchases

Tickets are purchased using funds held in your DRAWN wallet. Ticket prices are set per draw and displayed clearly before purchase. Each ticket gives you one entry into that draw.

The maximum number of tickets any single user may hold in any one draw is 25% of the total available tickets. This cap is enforced automatically.

4.2 Free postal entry

Every draw on DRAWN has a free postal entry route. No purchase is necessary to enter. To enter by post:

  1. Write a postcard including your full name, email address, the exact draw name, and the draw date.
  2. Post it to: DRAWN, PO Box 1000, London, EC1A 1BB
  3. The postcard must arrive by 5pm on the draw date.
  4. Standard UK postage applies. You are responsible for postage costs.
  5. One postcard equals one entry. Multiple postcards for the same draw will be counted as one entry.

Postal entries carry identical odds to purchased ticket entries and are entered into the same draw pool.

4.3 Draw thresholds

Each draw has a minimum ticket threshold (displayed on the draw page). If this threshold is not met by the draw closing time, the draw is cancelled and all ticket purchasers are automatically refunded in full to their DRAWN wallet.

4.4 Winner selection

Winners are selected using a cryptographically secure random number generator at 9pm UK time on the draw date. The outcome is mathematically random and cannot be influenced by DRAWN, sellers, or any third party. The live wheel animation shown in the app is for entertainment purposes only — the winner is determined algorithmically before the animation plays.

4.5 Draw cancellation

DRAWN reserves the right to cancel any draw at any time if there is evidence of fraud, a problem with the item, or any other reason that makes running the draw unfair or unlawful. In such cases all ticket purchasers will be fully refunded.

5. Wallet and payments

Wallet funds are held in a segregated account and are not used for DRAWN's operating expenses. They are available to you at all times for refund upon request, subject to any pending draw entries.

Minimum top-up amounts and any applicable fees are displayed at the point of purchase. DRAWN does not charge a fee for topping up your wallet.

Wallet balances are non-transferable between accounts and have no cash value except as set out in our refund policy below.

5.1 Refunds

Ticket purchases are non-refundable once a draw is live, except in cases where the draw is cancelled. If you change your mind before a draw goes live, contact us at support@drawn.app and we will do our best to accommodate you.

Wallet top-ups are refundable within 14 days if no tickets have been purchased with the funds, under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013.

Prizes cannot be exchanged for cash or swapped for an alternative item. The prize is as described in the draw listing.

9. Fraud — consequences and enforcement

Simply put: if you defraud DRAWN, buyers, or winners — you forfeit all money owed to you, we recover any losses from you, and we report you to the police. There are no exceptions.

9.1 Seller fraud

A seller commits fraud under these terms if they:

  • List an item they do not own or do not have the right to sell
  • List a counterfeit, stolen, or materially misrepresented item
  • Ship an item to DRAWN that differs from what was listed (different condition, different item, or missing components)
  • Attempt to cancel or reclaim an item after tickets have gone on sale
  • Collude with buyers or use fake accounts to purchase tickets in their own draw
  • Provide false identity or bank account information during seller onboarding

Where seller fraud is confirmed:

  • No payout will be made. Any funds held in escrow for that draw are forfeited entirely and redistributed as refunds to ticket buyers.
  • The seller's account is permanently closed with no right of appeal.
  • DRAWN will pursue recovery of any costs incurred — including item inspection, storage, return shipping, and administrative costs — directly from the seller.
  • DRAWN will report the matter to Action Fraud (the UK's national fraud reporting centre) and, where appropriate, to the Metropolitan Police.
  • Where a criminal offence has been committed (e.g. selling stolen goods, fraud by false representation under the Fraud Act 2006), DRAWN will provide all relevant evidence to law enforcement and cooperate fully with any prosecution.

9.2 Buyer fraud

A buyer commits fraud if they:

  • Use stolen payment details to top up their wallet
  • Create multiple accounts to circumvent the 25% ticket cap
  • Initiate a fraudulent chargeback after legitimately purchasing tickets
  • Attempt to claim a prize they did not legitimately win

Where buyer fraud is confirmed:

  • The account is permanently closed and all wallet funds are forfeited.
  • Any fraudulent chargebacks will be contested with full evidence provided to the card issuer.
  • DRAWN will report confirmed payment fraud to Action Fraud and the relevant card networks.
  • DRAWN reserves the right to pursue civil recovery of any losses suffered as a result of buyer fraud.

9.3 Investigation process

Where DRAWN suspects fraud, we will:

  1. Immediately freeze the relevant account(s) and any pending payouts.
  2. Conduct an internal investigation, which may take up to 14 days.
  3. Notify the affected party of our findings and give them 7 days to provide evidence in their defence.
  4. Make a final determination and communicate it in writing.

During an investigation, any draw involving a suspected fraudulent listing will be cancelled and all ticket buyers refunded in full. DRAWN will bear the cost of that cancellation — it will not be passed on to buyers.

9.4 Reporting fraud

If you suspect fraud — whether by a seller, buyer, or anyone else — please report it to us immediately at fraud@drawn.app. We take all reports seriously and investigate every one. Verified reports may be eligible for a reward at DRAWN's discretion.
